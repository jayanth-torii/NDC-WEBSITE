Refactor Admin Pages to Use EditorLayout and Tab Navigation

This commit introduces a major UI consistency update across the admin panel, transitioning multiple page editors from stacked or collapsible sections to a unified tab-based layout. 

Key changes include:
- Introduced `EditorLayout` and `TabCardSpec` across all major content editing pages.
- Replaced the legacy `CollapsibleSection` and vertically stacked `Panel` UI with a cleaner, horizontal tab-based navigation system (`tabKey` state).
- Standardized the use of `SectionHead` inside individual tab panels for better context and consistent aesthetics.
- Updated `PageEditor.tsx` (the generic editor) to use `EditorLayout`.
- Refactored specific, custom page editors to use tabs instead of stacked panels:
  - `AboutNdcPage.tsx`: Divided into Banner, Overview, Vision/Mission, Quality Policy, Milestones, and Affiliation sections.
  - `ActivitiesOverviewPage.tsx`: Separated Banner and Activity Cells sections.
  - `ActivityCellDetailPage.tsx`: Tabulated Banner, About/Overview, Faculty, Objectives, Activities list, and Gallery.
  - `AdmissionsPage.tsx`: Organized into Banner, How to Apply, and Eligibility tabs.
  - `AlumniPage.tsx`: Split into Banner, President's Message, Office Bearers, and Notable Alumni tabs.
  - `DepartmentsOverviewPage.tsx`: Separated into Banner and Departments list tabs.
  - `IicPage.tsx`: Resolved a parsing error in the JSX fragment structure and migrated to the tabbed layout.
  - `IqacPage.tsx`: Broken into Banner, About IQAC, Objectives, Functions, and Committee tabs.
  - `LibraryPage.tsx`: Categorized into Banner, About, Objectives, Advisory Committee, Sections, and E-Resources tabs.
  - `SamasthiPage.tsx`: Restructured into Banner, About, Features, Testimonials, Guidelines, and Useful Links tabs.
  - `SportsPage.tsx`: Organized into Banner, Vision/Mission, Director's Message, and Gallery tabs.

This structural refactor greatly improves usability for administrators by reducing vertical scrolling on content-heavy pages and ensuring a more predictable, modern editing experience that aligns with our shared design system.
